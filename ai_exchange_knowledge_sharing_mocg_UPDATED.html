Important balance

Reusable prompts are not universal forever

Revisit

Check if the prompt still fits your current process.

Retune

Adjust tone, structure, or emphasis when cases change.

Adapt

Different stakeholders and contexts need different outputs.

A good reusable workflow still needs periodic updates.
